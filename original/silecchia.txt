1. This is a review of the manuscript "Motion out a cannon" by Silecchia, Zucaro, and Bumshteyn, submitted as a research article to J S&E. I recommend major revisions.

2. The title needs to be more informative as to the actual findings. The abstract is nonspecific ("is affected by the launch angle" - HOW?) and has strange choices of word "proved how physics can be used"?

3. Introduction:
  a. Eq 1 makes no sense
  b. The introduction does not set up testable hypotheses. The paper should have been bench rejected for this.

4. Methods and materials focuses on roles people had when taking experimental data but is silent on analysis methods.

5. Results - results mixes results which should just be the factual findings and refer to figs 1 and 2 and discussion which explores the significance of the findings and how they weigh on the different alternative hypotheses (no hypotheses were provided).

6. "Discussion" says "we had a baseline idea that we could most likely use geometry..." why isn't this set up as a testable hypothesis, vis-a-vis Tartaglia's predicted trajectory shape. Etc. This discussion needs ot be completely redone.

7. NO references are provided!
